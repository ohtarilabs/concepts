On the repair work

What happens after you call.

The shop works with every insurance company and with self-pay customers. Either way, the order is the same.

  1. Call, or come by for a free estimate

    Bring the claim number if you have one; you do not need one to start.

  2. The shop works the claim with your insurer, or with you

    Insurance company assistance is part of the job, not an extra.

  3. Parts ordered, repair scheduled

    Towing can be arranged if the car cannot be driven in.

  4. Text updates while the work is done

    The thing customers mention most: they knew where the car was.

  5. Back to you, cleaned, under a lifetime warranty

    Cars go home washed inside and out, often ahead of the date given.
