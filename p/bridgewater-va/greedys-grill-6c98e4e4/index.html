The room, since October 2024.

Jose Dimas Hernandez came to Bridgewater from El Salvador fifteen years ago, learned kitchens from the inside, and already ran New York Flying Pizza up the street and Ciro's in Grottoes when Bob-A-Rea's owners retired in 2024. He bought the place that May, spent four weeks cleaning the kitchen and two months fitting it, and opened in October.

He kept the dishes people came in asking for, took the license plates off the walls, and told the Daily News-Record that what Bridgewater needed was something different from another pizza shop or burger place. The menu is his answer.

The building at 305 North Main has served food since Bob-A-Rea's opened in 1975.
