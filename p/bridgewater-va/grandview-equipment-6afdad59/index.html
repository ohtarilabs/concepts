Sales

Two lines, both on the floor to sit on and start: Husqvarna and Toro, from a homeowner's push mower to a commercial zero-turn. What is stocked, in the shop's own words:

  • Zero-turn mowersresidential and commercial
  • Lawn tractors
  • Chainsaws
  • Grass trimmers
  • Leaf blowers
  • Garden tillers
  • Automower robotic mowerssold and serviced here
  • Husqvarna
  • Toro

Tires too

The shop is also a full-service tire center: Firestone, Michelin, Cooper and BF Goodrich, and other lines at other prices, for the truck, the trailer and the tractor.

Parts

Blades, belts, filters, spark plugs, trimmer line, bar oil and chain, for the machines sold here and the ones that were not. Bring the model number, or the old part, and it gets matched at the counter; what is not on the shelf is ordered.

Before you come in

  • The model and serial numberon a plate under the seat or on the housing
  • The old part, if it came off
  • What the machine was doing when it stopped

Service

Tune-ups, blade sharpening, carburetor and fuel work, chain and bar, warranty repairs on both lines, and the machine that will not start in April. The technicians are factory trained and certified, by the shop's own account, and the work is done here, not sent away. Spring is the busy season, so call ahead of it.
