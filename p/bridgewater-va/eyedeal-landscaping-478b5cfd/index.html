1

Design

A design visit starts with a walk of the property and ends with a plan you can hold: the patio, the beds, the trees and the grades, drawn on the computer so you see the finished yard, and how the plants will look when they mature, before anything is bought or built.

2

Hardscapes

Walls, patios, driveways and walkways, designed and installed on a proper base so they stay level through Valley winters. The walk a customer wrote in about is the kind of job this is.

3

Grading

Full-service grading, seeding and sod: slopes reshaped, low spots filled and lawns started, so water runs away from the house instead of toward it. Grading is the part of a yard nobody photographs, and the part everything else depends on.

4

Maintenance

Mowing, trimming and edging, mulching, seeding and fertilizing, tree trimming and shrub removal, stump grinding, leaf removal in the fall and snow removal in the winter, for homes and businesses. Skid-loader work when a job needs it. The yard that was designed stays the yard that was built.
