Bridgewater College

The shop on South Main

219 South Main Street is a one-storey brick shop two doors from the Masonic Lodge; the National Register nomination for the town's historic district dates it to 1867 to 1869 and calls it a miniature of the lodge.

Judy Galang, Bridgewater College class of 1964, has kept Cottage Crafts here since 2002. She lives next door, which is why there are no hours: the shop opens when you call.
