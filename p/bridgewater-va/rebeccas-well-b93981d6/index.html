First Friday, on the grass.

From May to October, the first Friday evening of the month, the grounds behind the shop are Bridgewater's farmers market: growers, bakers, a beekeeper and a coffee stand under canopies, with Generations Park across the street.

At Rebecca's Well since 2024. The Town of Bridgewater posts the month's vendors: meats, produce, baked goods, honey, coffee. Bring cash; not every vendor takes cards.

Forty years in the house.

Becki Witt opened Rebecca's Well in December 1986 and has kept it since: seven rooms of a brick house built in the 1880s and 90s, across from Generations Park. Forty years in 2026.

The town's own directory describes it as "unique gifts and trinkets for just about anyone in your life", which is the right order of words: the person first, then the thing.

Listed by Rockingham County's business directory as seven rooms of gifts in a historic house, carrying Cat's Meow pieces, Rowe pottery and local antiques; in the 1984 Bridgewater Historic District inventory as a late-19th-century brick house on the north end of Main Street.
