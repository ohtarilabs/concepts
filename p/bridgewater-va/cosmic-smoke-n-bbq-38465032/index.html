A family on the trailer

Jeffrey Good started Cosmic under a tent in 2014 and moved it onto a trailer in 2017. He grew up in Bridgewater, graduated from Turner Ashby, served as a Navy Hospital Corpsman, and went full time on the pit in 2021. He lists his credentials plainly:

  • KCBSKansas City Barbeque Society life member, Certified BBQ Judge and Certified Table Captain
  • SCASteak Cookoff Association life member and certified judge
  • PCAPork Cookoff Association member and certified judge
  • MABAMid-Atlantic Barbecue Association member
  • ServSafeFood Protection Manager

Kelly Good, a nurse for thirty-one years, makes the sides and runs the events. Their grandson Addison Shank earned his ServSafe certificate at fifteen and works the window.

Find the trailer

On weekends the trailer serves barbecue sandwiches, barbecue nachos, sides and drinks from the window. Its stops are posted on Facebook: Saturday lots in Bridgewater, Food Truck Wednesdays in Verona, Friday plant stops in Timberville, summer concerts at Dove Park in Dayton and Endless Caverns in New Market, and the Open Doors Food Truck Fest at the Rockingham County Fairgrounds.
