Five years on the table, and a shop of her own.

Cierra Payne learned grooming from Gina Smiley at Gina's Pet Grooming in Waynesboro and opened Elite Edge on July 22, 2025, in the old Nan's New Look salon next to the 7-Eleven. The three dogs painted on the front of the building are hers: Loki, Squeak and Wrenn.

The speciality is Asian Fusion grooming, the rounded, teddy-bear style from Japan, China and Korea, alongside breed-standard work. She is a competitive groomer and travels to compete against groomers from across the country.

"We have multiple AKC certifications, including AKC S.A.F.E. Salon certification, jacketed coat type certification, curly coat certification, and more credentials towards a master groomer certification."

Cierra Payne, in the Bridgewater Buzz, August 2025
