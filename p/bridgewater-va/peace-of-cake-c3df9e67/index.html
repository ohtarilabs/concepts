From the stall

Inside North River Marketplace at 400 North Main Street, the stall keeps the marketplace's hours and sells what is baked that week:

  • Cupcakes, in flavours that rotate
  • Banana pudding
  • Chocolate mousse
  • Chocolate-dipped Oreos and pretzels
  • Vintage candy

Chalkboards too

The same hands that pipe the cakes draw chalkboard signs for businesses and events: a menu board, a welcome sign, a seating chart, a board for the dessert table. Ask when you order the cake, or ask on its own.

For the whole table

Birthdays, weddings, baby showers and company events: a cake, a tier of cupcakes, dipped pretzels in jars, candy in bowls, all from one kitchen. One June order was two thousand cupcakes for a credit union's member day. A wedding consult can cover the cake and the catering, because Rachal also runs events for Dayton Catering by day.

Find the stall

Walk in during the marketplace's hours for what is in the case. For a cake, call or write first; a custom order needs a few days, and a wedding needs a conversation.
