Three counters, one roof, twenty-five years

Simmons has cut, coloured, done nails and run tanning beds at 630 North Main Street, the 24/7 Family Fitness building at the top of Main Street, for more than twenty-five years. The stylists work for themselves under one name, which is why the prices say "from": the person who cuts your hair sets the number, and tells you before they start.

It is the kind of salon a student can come up from the college to for a $20 cut and a household can use for everything else. There are no packages at the hair and nail counters; you book the service you want and pay for that. Tanning is the one thing sold by the month.

"A full service hair, tanning and nail salon serving the Valley for over 25 years. Our stylists have over 150 years of combined experience."The salon's own line, from its page
