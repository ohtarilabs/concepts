When the mail has become a pile.

Kathy Rusmisel pays the bills, sorts the paperwork and sits with the lawyer and the accountant for people in Bridgewater and Rockingham County who cannot keep up with it, or would rather not. She comes to the house, once a month or as often as it takes.

What Kathy does

Every month

  • Reviews and pays the bills, and mails the paymentson time
  • Reconciles the bank accountsto the penny
  • Makes deposits, and signs cheques under a power of attorneywhen granted
  • Keeps a budget you can readone page
  • Visits, in personmonthly, or more

When something happens

  • Reads the medical bills and files the insurance claimsand follows up
  • Makes the phone calls and negotiates with creditorsso you need not
  • Organizes the records for the accountant at tax timeevery year
  • Prepares correspondence for your signatureas needed

When asked

  • Serves as trustee or executorif requested
  • Prepares probate materials and sees the family through probatestep by step
  • Notarizes documentsa notary public
  • Sits in on care-planning meetings and speaks for you with the professionalsan advocate
  • Stores the bills and documentsthree years

Who this is for

  • A parent who cannot keep up any more.The bills arrive; the cheques do not go out; the late notices start.
  • A daughter or son who lives two hours away.Someone in town who sees the mail every month and calls you when something is off.
  • After a loss, a diagnosis or a divorce.When the person who handled the money is gone, or it is suddenly one person's job.
  • Anyone who wants their evenings back.New to the workforce, newly married, or just done with the stack on the counter.

How it starts

Kathy worked at Leitner Law Group, an elder-law practice in Harrisonburg, before this, and did all of it for her own family, unpaid, for twenty-four years. She works from Bridgewater and drives to her clients.

  • A call, and you describe what is going wrongten minutes
  • A visit to the house to see the mail and the accountsan hour
  • A plan: what she takes over, what stays with youin writing
  • The monthly visit, and a phone call when something needs onefrom then on
