The Virginia state inspection, item by item.

Every car registered in Virginia needs a safety inspection once a year, and the sticker has to come from a licensed station. This is what the inspector is looking at.

Brakes and parking brake

Headlights and high beams

Turn signals and brake lights

Tires, wheels and tread depth

Steering and suspension

Exhaust system

Windshield, glass and wipers

Mirrors

Horn

Seat belts

Fuel system and hoses

Hood latch, doors and floor pan

Items follow the Virginia State Police safety inspection rules; the full list is longer and is set by the state, not the shop.

Customers describe the shop the same way across fifteen years of reviews: honest, no upsell, and willing to fit you in.

Since 1972, on Main Street.

Wayne Hussey and Mary Speakes opened the shop in 1972. Their son Dusty took over in 1989 and is the one at the counter today; the reviews from the last two years name him, and the oldest customers count their time here in decades rather than visits.

The reviews from this year say what the ones from 2011 said: prompt, fair on price, and honest about what needs doing. One customer drove fifty-five miles from Strasburg three times for the work and the price.

Fisher Auto Parts is at 118 North Main, down the same street, so most of what a car needs is within a few blocks.
