First of sixteen.

Tripadvisor ranks Francesco's first of the sixteen restaurants it lists in Bridgewater, from 112 reviews, as of September 2026.

The restaurant opened in 2008 at the north end of Main Street: 120 seats at 27 tables, a bar and a patio. Dave Miller, who ran Dave's Downtown Taverna in Harrisonburg from 1994 to 2013, bought it in January 2022. The Dave's Taverna pitas on the menu carry his old restaurant's name.

The weekday lunch buffet

Pizza, pasta and a salad bar, 13.50, and 6.00 under ten. The buffet came back on February 7, 2022, after the pandemic took it away, and it has been the lunch crowd's reason to come ever since.
