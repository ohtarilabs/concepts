The Barbee House, about 1840.

Brick, two storeys, and a porch on four stuccoed columns with a sawn balustrade above. The National Register nomination for the Bridgewater Historic District calls it one of the oldest and most imposing houses on the north end of Main Street. By the late 1800s Colonel Gabriel Barbee was running it as an inn called the Barbee House, and the name stayed.

After the Civil War the house was a girls' boarding school; the room numbers are still on the doors upstairs. Patti and Tim Landes bought it in late 2015, after more than a century as a family home, and opened the pub in 2017 with the outside much as it was. The name is the porch's own: one of the four pillars carries two long cracks.

Bridgewater Historic District, Virginia Landmarks Register and National Register of Historic Places, listed 1984. Barbee House, 403 North Main Street.
