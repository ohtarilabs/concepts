What a renovation actually involves

People are usually surprised that the pool itself stays. The shell is the expensive part and it is almost always fine. Everything you can see is what gets replaced.

  • 01

    Drain and strip

    Old plaster comes off, old tile comes off, and we find out what the shell is really like underneath. Anything structural gets discussed before it gets fixed.

  • 02

    Plumbing and equipment

    The right moment to deal with lines, returns, the pump and the heater, because the water is out and the deck is open.

  • 03

    Tile and coping

    New waterline tile and new coping stones. This is the part everybody's eye actually lands on.

  • 04

    Surface

    New plaster or an aggregate finish. This is what makes the water look the colour it looks.

  • 05

    Deck

    Pavers, stone or a resurfaced slab. Often the difference between a repaired pool and a new garden.

What does it cost?

Most companies in this trade will not answer that until they are standing in your garden. We would rather tell you what actually drives the number, so you can work out roughly where you sit before you ring anybody.

  • Whether the deck is being replaced or kept
  • Plaster, or an aggregate finish
  • How much tile, and which tile
  • Whether the plumbing and equipment come with it
  • What we find once the old surface is off
