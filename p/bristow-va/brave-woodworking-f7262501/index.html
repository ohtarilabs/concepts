How a job actually goes

First visit

We measure the wall

Every wall is out of square somewhere. We find out by how much before anything is drawn, which is where the sixteenth of an inch comes from.

Then

We draw it

You see the piece in the actual opening, with the real dimensions, before we cut anything.

Then

We build it

Made on Redoubt Road in Manassas. Nothing is ordered in and assembled on your floor.

Last

We install and finish

Scribed to the wall, trimmed, and finished so it reads as part of the house.

Send us the wall you cannot solve.

A photograph and a rough measurement is enough to start. We will tell you what is possible in the space and what it would take, before anybody commits to anything.
