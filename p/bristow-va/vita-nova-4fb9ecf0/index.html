And the coffee, which is why people stay

Seasonal drinks, frozen options, a long list of loose leaf teas, and pastries and snacks from people nearby. There are gluten free and vegan options because we would rather everybody got a treat. There is a small play area for the energetic ones, and nobody is going to move you along.

Wander the shop, linger. That is the whole idea, and it is why the coffee bar and the furniture share a room instead of a wall.

Come out to Fitzwater Drive

We are at 13051 Fitzwater Drive in Nokesville, out where Route 28 meets Fitzwater. Early enough on weekdays to catch you before work, and open late enough that an evening browse is a real option. Call the shop on (703) 594-0111.
