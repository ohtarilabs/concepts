What a visit actually looks like

The part people want to know before they call a mechanic they found on the internet is not whether he is any good. It is what happens when he turns up.

  1. You text me what the car is doing

    Noise, light on the dash, something that started last Tuesday. Photos help. So does the year and the mileage.

  2. I tell you what it will cost before I start

    You get the number first. If it turns out to be something else once I am in there, you hear about it before I carry on, not after.

  3. I come to you

    Your driveway, your office car park, wherever the car is sitting. You can stand there and watch the whole thing if you want to. Most people do, the first time.

  4. You pay when it is done

    Car finished, in your own driveway, and nobody drove it anywhere but you.

What I come out for

Why I started doing it this way

I spent about ten years working in auto shops. I got tired of working for places that took advantage of the people who walked in. When Covid hit and nobody wanted to sit in a waiting room anyway, it seemed like the right moment to go out on my own and bring the work to people instead.

So there is no waiting room, no service adviser, and no upsell sitting between you and the person doing the work. It is just me, in your driveway, and you can see exactly what I am doing to your car.
