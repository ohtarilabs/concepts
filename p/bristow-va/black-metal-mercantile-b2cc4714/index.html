A little of something for everyone

What makes general stores great is that they have a little of something for everyone. Food and drinks, home decor, collectibles, music, leather journals, flasks, hot sauces and seasonings, candles, toys, crafts, candy, plush animals, stickers, magnets, art prints, Virginia peanuts, and a good many locally handcrafted items from local artisans.

We love unique, we love distinct, and we love sharing all of the fun and interesting things we find with everyone who comes through the door.

The shop was founded by Deron Blevins, a Manassas native whose working life ran from civil-engineering draftsman to graphic designer to web developer, by way of co-founding an award-winning candle company. Black Metal Mercantile is what happens when all of that gets pointed at a storefront on Center Street.
