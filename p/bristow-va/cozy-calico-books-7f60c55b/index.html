Events here are free and open to anyone. Buying the book at the shop is never required, and it is what pays for the chairs, so if you can, buy it here rather than somewhere else. The Fall Craft Show dates and the shop's own author schedule are real; the rest of this block is shown as a shape for the concept.

Checkers works Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

He does not help. He sits on the windowsill in the afternoon sun and occasionally on a book somebody was about to buy, and he is the reason a fair number of people come in the first time.

The shop is named for a calico, which is both a cat and a printed cotton, and either reading is fine.
