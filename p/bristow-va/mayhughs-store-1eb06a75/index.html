The same crossroads since 1912.

Mayhugh's has stood at the corner of Greenwich Road and Vint Hill Road since 1912. The sign out front still names the two things people drive out for. The rest of the store is what a household actually runs out of: groceries, garden and feed, tackle, and a fuel island that opens at six.

Nokesville, Virginia. A railroad stop since 1865, about sixteen hundred people, and the same store on the corner since 1912.
