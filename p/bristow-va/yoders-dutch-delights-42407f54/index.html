Two of five, from Central Pennsylvania

We grew up as the youngest two of five siblings in an Amish-Mennonite community in Central Pennsylvania. From our parents we learned the value of home-cooked meals, strong family ties and hard work.

When we left our childhood community we entered the real world with no connections, no formal experience and no college degrees. What we did have was work ethic, resilience and a determination to build something of our own.

In 2018 we bought a food truck and a cherished donut recipe from our cousin at Mrs. Yoder's Kitchen in Richmond. After six years of farmers markets, festivals and private events across Northern Virginia, we opened our first brick-and-mortar shop in Manassas Park in February 2025. Our cousin Kimberly runs it with us.

Sam and Jules Yoder

It feels crazy to think about the days we did fifty dollars, and now we have the shop.

The recipe is sourdough and yeast with very little sugar in the dough, which is why ours come out of the fryer looking like a cake donut and eating like something lighter. The sweetness is in the glaze and the filling, not the ring.

The truck still runs. Find us at the Manassas City farmers market on Saturdays and the Dale City farmers market on Sundays.

Jules Yoder, quoted in the Prince William Times, March 2025
