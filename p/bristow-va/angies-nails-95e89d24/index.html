Fairy Hair, and what it actually is.

Twenty-five fine strands tied individually at the root, so they move with your hair rather than sitting on top of it. Thirty-five dollars, and twenty-five for children.

It takes about as long as a manicure, and it is the one thing here nobody arrives knowing the price of. So it is printed above with everything else.
