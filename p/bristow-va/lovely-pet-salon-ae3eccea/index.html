Lovely Pet Salon, in their own words

Some dogs walk in and hop on the table. Some need ten minutes on the floor first, and some need to come back a second time before they will let anyone near their feet. We groom both kinds. If your dog is nervous, elderly, or has had a bad haircut somewhere else, tell us when you call and we will book you the extra time rather than rush you through it.

Before you come

How long will it take?

Most full grooms run two to three hours. We will give you a pickup time when you drop off, and we call you if it moves.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, for grooming. Both shops book by phone so we can ask about your dog's coat and temperament before you arrive.

What should I bring?

Nothing. If your dog has a shampoo they need for a skin condition, bring that and tell us why.

Boarding. If you need someone to watch your dog while you are away, ask us when you call. We keep it small.
