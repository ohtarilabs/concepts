You do not need to know anything to come in.

People stand outside this shop and talk themselves out of it, which is a shame, because the most common thing said inside it is a question. Nobody here is going to test you.

If you came for a candle, buy the candle. If you came because something is going on and a friend said we were kind, that is also a reason, and it is the one we hear most.

What is on the shelves.

Incense blended here rather than bought in, loose herbs by weight, candles, stones, jewellery and tarot. If you do not know what you want, say so and somebody will walk the shelves with you.
