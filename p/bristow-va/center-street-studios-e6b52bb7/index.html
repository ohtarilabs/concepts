Before your first class

When should I get there?
Ten minutes early the first time, so there is room to talk through anything that hurts before the class starts rather than during it.
What should I wear?
Something you can move in that is not so loose the teacher cannot see how you are moving. Grip socks help on the reformer and are not required.
What if I am coming back from an injury, or pregnant?
Say so when you book. Modification is the ordinary way this studio works, not a special accommodation.
How do I get in touch?
The studio number takes texts rather than calls, which most people find easier for a first question anyway.
