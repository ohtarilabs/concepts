The Golden Plum

Formerly Mom's Apple Pie in Occoquan. Same kitchen, same recipes, same family.

Avis and Steve Cox baked here for twenty years. In 2023 their daughter Ansa took the shop on, remodelled it and changed the sign. Nothing else changed.

Call ahead for the cinnamon rolls.

They are made fresh daily and they usually sell out while the day's orders are still coming in. One is $5.50, a box of four is $20, half a dozen is $30. Ring the shop and one will be waiting.

Keeping it. A whole pie holds three or four days in the refrigerator and two months in the freezer. Take a cream pie straight home. Warm a fruit pie for ten minutes before you cut it and nobody will know it was not baked that hour.

Mom's Breads are still here.

The Sunflower Crunch Loaf never left the shelf and it never left the recipe book. Six dollars, same loaf, same method, baked in the same room it has been baked in for twenty years.

So are the Pulla, the Challah and the Irish Soda. If you used to buy your bread here on a Saturday morning, you still can.
