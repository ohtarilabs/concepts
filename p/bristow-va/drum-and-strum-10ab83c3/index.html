Sell it, fix it, rent it, teach it

Four trades out of one address on Main Street, which is the reason people who could order a guitar to their door drive to Warrenton instead.

  • The bench

    Setups, fretwork, cracks, electronics. The part of this shop that cannot be shipped to you, and the reason an instrument bought here plays properly when it gets home.

  • The floor

The Gloria Faye Dingus Music Alliance

Gloria Faye Dingus founded this shop in 1990 with her husband Gary and their children Cheryl and Tim. The Alliance carries her name, and it puts instruments and music instruction into the hands of children who could not otherwise have them.

It began in the listening room at the back of this building, with forty chairs and a stage. In 2018 it opened Gloria's, a performing arts centre of its own further along Main Street. The shop and the Alliance have been the same idea from the start: get instruments into people's hands and give them somewhere to play them.

102 Main Street

We are in Old Town Warrenton, and we have been at this address since 1990. Come and take something off the wall. If it needs work, the bench is here too, and so is somebody who can tell you whether it is worth doing.
