How a job moves through the shop

Six steps, in the order they happen. Most jobs clear all six in 24 to 48 hours.

  1. 1

    Order

    Send the file, or tell the counter the job and the quantity.

  2. 2

    Design

    The in-house designer sets it up, or builds it from scratch.

  3. 3

    Proof

    You see it before it prints, and nothing runs until you say so.

  4. 4

    Print

    On the shop's own press, on the stock you picked.

  5. 5

    Finish

    Cutting, folding, stitching or laminating, as the job needs.

  6. 6

    Deliver

    Pick it up at the counter, or the van brings it, free within 45 minutes.

Three generations in Nokesville

George Reaves Sr. ran an appliance and electronics store in Nokesville in the 1950s and 60s, and later the Brentsville Superette, a little country store on the family's land just outside town. His son, George Reaves Jr., spent forty years in the paper supply business before buying Nokesville Print and Copy in 2018, on Main Street, across from the Post Office.

After George Jr.'s death, his daughter Shelly reopened the shop as Nokesville Printing Solutions LLC to finish the expansion he had planned. She bought the Century Press of Manassas book of business and brought its owner, with thirty-five years at the press, into the shop.

It is the third print shop on Main Street in twenty years, and the neighbours like having one in town.

A father's plan, a daughter's shop, and a printer with thirty-five years of ink on his hands.
