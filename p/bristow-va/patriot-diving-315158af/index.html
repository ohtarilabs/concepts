How far down do you want to go?

Every certification is a rung, and they run in order. Find the one you are on. If you have never breathed off a regulator, that is the first one, and it happens in a pool.

  • Discover Scuba Diving

    40 ft

    from $100

    No certification, no commitment. One session with an instructor and you are breathing underwater. Most people book this one first and decide afterward.

  • Open Water Diver

    60 ft

    $880

    The one that makes you a diver. It does not expire and it works anywhere in the world you go on holiday.

  • Advanced Open Water

    100 ft

    Five dives, five new skills, including navigation and your first proper deep dive. No exam.

  • Rescue Diver

    100 ft

    Not deeper. Harder. Divers who have taken it tend to describe it as the course that changed how they dive.

  • Divemaster and Instructor

    130 ft

    The working end of the ladder. We run the full Instructor Development Course here, which is what the Five Star IDC rating means and what most shops this size cannot offer.

Depths shown are the standard recreational limits for each certification. The Open Water price includes confined water gear, and a wetsuit, jacket and regulator for the open water dives; mask, snorkel, boots and fins are yours to buy or rent. There is a military discount. Ask for it on the phone. Been out of the water a while? ReActivate is a short refresher, not a new course. We also teach Adaptive Support Diver, stay dry certifications, Bubblemaker and Seal Team for children, and four levels of mermaid.

Adaptive diving is taught here.

Patriot Diving teaches Handicapped Scuba Association courses. Allie, who runs the shop with Roger, holds a seat on the board of Patriots for Disabled Divers.

Walter Reed, Fort Belvoir and Quantico are all inside a forty minute drive of Mill Street. If you or someone you dive with needs the course adapted, call and ask for the detail. It is a conversation, not a form.

We service all gear.

Regulators, jackets, computers, tanks. Bring it in before the trip rather than after it, and bring it in even if you bought it somewhere else.

Air and nitrox fills, and rentals for a class or for a weekend. If you are not sure what you need yet, do not buy anything: take the pool session first and ask afterward.

Some of you know us as Patriot Scuba.

In early 2023 Jeff and Merial decided to leave the retail dive business, and Roger and Allie took the shop on rather than let it close. Roger had managed it. Allie had been diving out of it for years.

Same shop, same pool, same weekends in the water. If you certified with Patriot Scuba, your records and your instructors are still here, and the pool sessions still run on the same nights.
