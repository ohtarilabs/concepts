Madigan's Waterfront

201 Mill Street, Occoquan

(703) 494-6373

You can get here by boat.

Seafood and steaks on the Occoquan River, with a dining room, a bar, a deck over the water and a private room upstairs. Tie up at the dock and walk in.

  • Public boat docking and dock rentals
  • Brunch Saturday and Sunday from 10:30
  • Book on Tock, or call for seven or more
  • Happy hour every day

Four rooms, four ways in.

They are not the same evening and they do not work the same way, so here is how you get a table in each one.

The dining room

Seafood and steaks, white tablecloths, the river through the window.

Book on Tock, one to six. Seven or more, call the host.

The bar

Big room, televisions, happy hour every day, live music and karaoke.

No booking. Open from 11am to last call.

The deck and tiki bar

Out over the water, with fire pits. The best seat here, and the one everybody wants.

First come, weather permitting. Reservations cover inside only.

Upstairs

A private room with its own bar, its own deck and a dance floor.

Private parties. Call and ask for the banquet packet.
