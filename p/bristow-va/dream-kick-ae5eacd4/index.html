What a class is like

The school's own line is "self-confidence through self-defense training", and a class is built to earn it. Students bow in, warm up, and work through the parts of WTF Tae Kwon Do in order.

  • BasicsStances, blocks, strikes and the kicks the style is known for, drilled in lines.
  • FormsSet patterns of movement, learned one at a time as a student moves up.
  • SparringControlled and padded, with tournament classes for students who want to compete.
  • BreakingBoards, at the right moments, and at birthday parties.
