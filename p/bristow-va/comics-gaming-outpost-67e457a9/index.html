The room is the point

Plenty of places will sell you a booster box. What you cannot order to your door is four other people, a table, and somebody behind the counter who knows the rules well enough to settle it.

That is what the back of this shop is for. Bring a deck, bring a board game, or turn up with nothing and we will find you a seat at something.

What is on the shelves

And if it is a comic we do not have, we will order it in for you. People seem to mention that one a lot.

    A lot of them, in a lot of variety, and a pull list if you want one.

Never played any of this before?

That is the most common way people walk in here, and it is genuinely fine. Tell whoever is behind the counter what you are curious about and roughly who it is for. Nobody is going to sell you a four-hundred-page rulebook when a starter set would do.
