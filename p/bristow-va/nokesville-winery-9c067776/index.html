Start with the Norton

Norton is the grape Virginia can call its own, and it is the vine Tara and Dustin planted first. Ours grows on the two acres around the winery and again in Middleburg. If you only taste one thing, taste this.

Tara and Dustin

Nearly twenty years ago they started visiting Virginia wineries on weekends. By the mid-2010s they had planted vines. Before they had a winery, they made wine wherever they could, even in the basement.

Vintage after vintage, the hobby became the calling. Tara took on building the family dream; Dustin took to the balance of science and craft in the winemaking. The winery grew from a converted shed on the Nokesville acres to a tasting room on Fitzwater Drive, and the vineyards grew from one site to three.

It is still a small farm winery. The people pouring on a Thursday are the people who pruned in February.

From the winery's own story, told at the bar and on the About page.

Two ways to join

Both clubs are built around bottles you choose yourself. One is for people who want the winery delivered; the other is for people who are already here most weeks.

Ville Club

For the regular who wants the good stuff first, and delivered.

To start
A one-time $60 initiation plus four bottles.
Each quarter
Four bottles you select, $85 to $153 plus tax.
Discount
15% off wine and events bought at the winery.
Delivery
Free within Prince William County or twenty miles of the winery; pick up at the winery if you prefer.
Extras
Quarterly pick-up parties, first call on new releases, small-lot member-only wines, no deposit on private events, and two glasses, two flights or up to four tastings every visit.

Cork Club

For the neighbour who drops in on Thursdays and would like it to count.

To start
Any three bottles.
Every three months
Two more bottles keep the membership active.
Points
20 member points each period, spent at the bar on glasses, flights and tastings.
Discount
10% off every visit.
Joining
Ask at the bar. That is the whole form.
