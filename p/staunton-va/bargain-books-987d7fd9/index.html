What is in there.

More than you can get through in an afternoon, which is the point. Nothing here is catalogued online, so the honest answer to "do you have it" is usually "probably, come and look."

Fiction

The largest section by a distance. Paperbacks, hardbacks, whole runs of an author on one shelf.

History

Military, American, local Virginia. The kind of depth that only comes from twenty years of estate boxes.

Children's and young adult

Picture books through to teen series. At these prices you can let a child pick their own without wincing.

Comics and graphic novels

Single issues and collected editions, boxed and browsable.

Vinyl and CDs

Crates of used LPs to flip through, plus CDs by the shelf. Rock, country, classical, soundtracks.

DVD, Blu-Ray, VHS and cassettes

Including the formats nobody else still carries. If you kept the player, we kept the tapes.

Audio books

Boxed sets and single titles, for the drive down the valley.

Crystals

Yes, really. They are near the front, and they are worth a look on the way past.

We trade books.

Bring a box. Bring the car. If you are clearing a house, moving, or simply out of shelf, we will look at what you have and trade against it. Call the store before you load up anything larger than a car boot so somebody is there to help you carry it in.
