Twenty years on North Augusta Street.

Bicycle sales and service in Staunton since 2005. Anything with two wheels, from a child's tricycle to a full suspension trail bike to an e-bike that has stopped charging.

The bench.

Most of what comes through the door is not a new bike. It is a bike somebody already owns and wants to keep riding. Call or stop in for an estimate; these are the jobs we do most.

Flat repairWhile you wait Tube, rim tape, and a look at what put the hole there in the first place.
Tune-up2 to 3 days Shifting and brakes adjusted, drivetrain cleaned, wheels trued, bolts checked to torque.
Wheel truing and rebuildsBy appointment Trued in the stand, tensioned evenly, dished true. Broken spokes replaced, or a full rebuild on your hub.
Tubeless setup and conversionSame day Tape, valves, sealant, seated and checked. Bring the wheels or bring the bike.
Suspension serviceAsk us Lowers, seals and air can service, or sent out for a full damper rebuild if that is what it needs.
E-bike diagnosticsBring the charger Motor, battery and controller faults on Aventon and Specialized systems, plus the ordinary bicycle half that most shops forget an e-bike still has.
Kids' bikes and tricyclesUsually while you wait Seat height, training wheels, a chain back on, brakes that a child can actually pull. No charge for the small ones.
