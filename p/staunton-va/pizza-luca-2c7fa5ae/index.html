Everything comes out of the same fire.

Dough is stretched by hand and the pies go straight onto the brick. That is where the blistering on the rim comes from, and it is why the Spicy Soppressata Salami and Hot Honey tastes the way it does.

An old showroom on N. Lewis Street.

Staunton's Historic Auto Architecture District is a rare thing: a downtown block built for the first years of the automobile, and still standing. Pizza Luca sits inside one of those buildings.

Tall windows, hard floors, a room that carries sound. It fills up early on Friday and it is worth calling ahead.
